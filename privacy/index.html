SYNAPZ: Swarm Protocol — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

SYNAPZ: Swarm Protocol ("the game") is published by SYNAPZ AI LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example shards, gems, upgrades, unlocked sectors and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

If the game offers optional in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded so the item can be delivered. If we later use a purchase-validation service, we will update this policy before it goes live.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Test versions

Beta builds distributed through TestFlight or as test downloads follow this same policy. Apple may collect crash and usage data under its own TestFlight privacy terms, which you can control in the TestFlight app.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
